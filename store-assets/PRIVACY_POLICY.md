# Privacy Policy — PDF Reader

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

PDF Reader ("the app") is developed by an independent developer. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Summary
The app works entirely on your device. It does **not** collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## Your documents
When you open a PDF, the app keeps a private copy inside its own app storage so you can reopen it later from your library. These copies never leave your device unless you choose to share or print them. Removing a document from the library, clearing the library, or uninstalling the app deletes these copies. Your original files are never modified.

## Permissions
The app does not request access to your photos, media, contacts, camera, microphone or location. Files are opened only through the Android system file picker, or when you choose "Open with" or "Share" from another app.

## Passwords
Passwords for protected PDFs are used only to open the document on your device and are never saved or transmitted.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
