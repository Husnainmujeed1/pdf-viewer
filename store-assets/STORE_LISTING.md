## Full description (max 4000 characters)
PDF Reader is a fast, clean and private PDF viewer for Android. Open any PDF from your phone, from email or WhatsApp, or from any app with "Open with" or "Share", and pick up exactly where you left off.

★ KEY FEATURES
• Fast, smooth PDF rendering with pinch-to-zoom and double-tap zoom
• Remembers your last page in every document automatically
• Bookmarks: save important pages and jump back to them in one tap
• Table of contents: browse chapters and sections in documents that have them
• Page slider and "Go to page" for very large documents
• Vertical or horizontal scrolling, plus page-by-page mode
• Night reading mode (inverted colors) and warm eye-comfort mode
• Keep the screen on while reading
• Password-protected PDF support
• Favorites, search, and sorting by name, size, date added or recently opened
• Share and print documents
• Clickable links inside PDFs
• Light and dark themes
• Full-screen reading: tap the page to hide the toolbars

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works completely offline
• No account or sign-up
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics
• No access to your photos or media. You choose exactly which files to open.

Simple, lightweight and focused on reading. Download PDF Reader today!
